How to play

The goal

Fruit roll in from the left and try to escape off the right edge. Every fruit that gets out costs lives. Survive all 30 waves!

Build your own lanes

There is no fixed road. Fruit always take the shortest open route, so place walls (cheap!) and towers to build a long, twisty maze. You can never fully block the path. The glowing arrows show the route the fruit will take, and while placing something you see the new route before you commit.

Drop a tower on top of a wall to turn that wall into a tower. You get the wall's cost back.

Skin hardness

Every fruit's toughness comes from how hard its skin is in real life. Soft raspberries pop in one hit, but coconuts have huge HP and armor that blocks damage from every hit. Use the Peeler to strip armor, the Skewer to pierce it, and fire to cook straight through it.

Multiplayer

Host a game and share the 4-letter code (or invite link). Everyone defends the same kitchen: lives are shared, each chef has their own gold, and every chef earns gold from every squashed fruit. Waves start when all chefs are ready.

Fruit Almanac

Toughness comes from real-world skin hardness. HP grows with hardness1.75 and armor with hardness2. Armor is subtracted from every hit (a hit always deals at least 20%).
